# Public Content Rules

## Publicly appropriate
The public site may describe THE LISTEN MODEL™; display the six-letter orientation; explain high-level philosophy, scope, ethics, applications, training, and pilot participation; and use selected conceptual material approved for public use.

## Participant / controlled learning
Pilot materials may add guided foundational practice, reflection, introductory noise awareness, role awareness, and structured application. Access level should be increased before publishing deeper participant workbooks or saved reflections.

## Advanced / protected
Do not publish the full concurrent-lens methodology, Support–Readiness Alignment Grid, detailed practitioner case mechanics, certification scoring/decision standards, misuse thresholds, facilitator-only interpretation, licensing controls, or other protected advanced LMBOK material as open website content.

## Integrity rule
Do not describe the advanced lenses as contradictory to the acronym. They are a different, deeper approach for advanced education. Do not describe Presence / Interpretation / Clarification / Reflection / Response as the L.I.S.T.E.N. acronym.
