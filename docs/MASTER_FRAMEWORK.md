- **N — Navigate the Support Strategy**

## Governing philosophy
LISTEN is a discipline, not a technique to be performed on another person. Public education may teach the orientation and foundational listening practices, but must not convert the model into a mechanical checklist, diagnostic instrument, decision tree, or outcome-control script.

Core commitments preserved from the LMBOK source material include: listening precedes influence; understanding precedes guidance; trust precedes transformation; presence before response; clarity before action; restraint before influence; autonomy, role clarity, boundaries, and responsible support.

## Supporting foundational practices
Presence, Interpretation, Clarification, Reflection, and Response are retained as **supporting educational practices**. They are not what the letters L.I.S.T.E.N. stand for.

## Advanced / protected education
The LMBOK includes deeper practitioner education such as concurrent listening lenses (Need, Language, Goal, Style & Preference, and the 3 C's), Noise standards, Self-LISTEN, Support–Readiness alignment, case analysis, certification standards, misuse thresholds, and licensing governance. These are not public website mechanics.
